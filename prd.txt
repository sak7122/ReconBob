Project Title: Agentic Bookkeeping & Receipt Auditor for TradespeopleTarget Category: Small Business ServicesHackathon: Build with Gemini XPRIZE1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition1.1 ObjectiveTo build an AI-native, zero-UI financial operations agent that automates bookkeeping, receipt reconciliation, and expense auditing for independent tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, contractors). The system operates entirely within WhatsApp/SMS, removing the friction of manual data entry and complex accounting software.1.2 The ProblemTradespeople lose thousands of dollars annually in unclaimed tax deductions, double-billed merchant invoices, and unbilled materials because they track expenses using paper receipts thrown into truck dashboards. Existing software (QuickBooks, Xero) requires disciplined manual upkeep, mobile app navigation, and desktop alignment—tasks busy contractors avoid.1.3 The SolutionAn autonomous multi-agent system built on Google Cloud and powered by Gemini 1.5 Pro. It acts as a proactive virtual accountant. Users interact solely through text messaging by snapping photos of receipts. The AI handles line-item extraction, matches expenses to live bank feeds, detects billing anomalies, and reconciles records autonomously.2. User Personas & User Experience (UX)2.1 Persona: "Dave the Plumber"Characteristics: Constantly on the move, drives a truck between jobs, manages 1–2 apprentices, handles billing at night or on weekends, hates software.Core Need: Wants to ensure every business expense is logged for tax write-offs without spending more than 5 seconds doing it.2.2 Core User Flows (Zero-UI Architecture)Flow A: Real-time Receipt SubmissionDave purchases copper pipes at Home Depot and receives a paper receipt.While walking to his truck, Dave opens WhatsApp and snaps a photo of the receipt to the project's dedicated business number.The AI processes the image and replies within 10 seconds:"Got it! Logged $143.50 at Home Depot for Plumbing Supplies. Noticed a $12 charge for a tape measure—should I categorize that as Tools?"Dave replies: "Yes." The transaction is fully reconciled.Flow B: Proactive Reconciliation Loop (The Agentic Trigger)The AI syncs with Dave’s bank feed and detects a charge of $320.00 from a local electrical supply distributor with no corresponding receipt.The AI initiates a WhatsApp message:"Hey Dave, I see a charge for $320.00 at 'City Electric' from yesterday morning, but I don't have a receipt for it. Can you snap a photo or text me what it was for?"Dave texts back a photo or replies: "That was for the Smith job wire."The AI updates the ledger and creates an internal dummy receipt/memo for his accountant.3. 3.1 Multi-Agent BreakdownAgent 1: Multimodal Ingestion & OCR AgentEngine: Gemini 1.5 Pro via Vertex AI.Role: Parses incoming image attachments and text messages.Task: Leverage Gemini's native multimodal capabilities to extract raw structured JSON from crumpled, grease-stained, or blurry receipt images. It extracts: Merchant, Date, Subtotal, Tax, Total, and individual Line Items (SKUs and descriptions).Agent 2: Bank Reconciliation AgentEngine: Gemini 1.5 Flash.Role: Matches extracted receipts against incoming bank transaction data streams (simulated via Plaid API sandbox).Task: Run a deterministic fuzzy-matching algorithm augmented by LLM reasoning to pair a receipt with a bank debit within a $\pm 3$-day window.Agent 3: Anomaly Detection & Fraud AuditorEngine: Gemini 1.5 Pro.Role: Evaluates historical pricing trends and supplier behaviors.Task: Compare current line-item costs against historical data. (e.g., If Home Depot charged $4.50 per unit of copper pipe last week, but a different branch charged $6.20 this week, flag the variance to the user).3.2 Data Storage & LoggingDatabase: Google Cloud Firestore to track user states, session variables, mapped transactions, and agent metadata.Agent Visibility: Implement rigorous logging inside Cloud Logging to capture step-by-step agent execution histories (required for hackathon product evidence submissions).4. Functional Requirements4.1 Core Features (MVP Scope)Multimodal Text-to-Bookkeeper: Acceptance of JPG, PNG, and HEIC images via WhatsApp/SMS.Line-Item Splitter: Ability to separate personal items from business items on a single receipt based on text instructions from the user.Plaid Integration Simulation: Automated daily ingestion of bank feed transactions to compare against logged images.Smart Prompting Engine: Proactive, context-aware notification loops to ask users for missing inputs.CSV/QuickBooks Export: A simple command (/export) that texts back a secure download link containing an accountant-ready Excel/CSV sheet mapped to standard tax categories (Schedule C compliance).4.2 Non-Functional RequirementsLatency: End-to-end processing from receipt image submission to WhatsApp confirmation reply must take less than 15 seconds.Security: All user data, PII, and financial transaction references must be encrypted at rest and in transit within Google Cloud.5.
